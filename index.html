Invoice Creator Guide

Quickly issue simple invoices

Use this tab to create quick sales receipts for standard exhibition items: Sarees, Coord Sets, Cotton Dress, and Heavy Dress.

  • Simply type the quantity and rate for the item categories sold.
  • Leave quantity as 0 for items not purchased.
  • On submit, the customer will be automatically added to the **Visitor Registry** if their phone number is not already registered.
  • The invoice is logged in the **Stall Sales** history and can be viewed or printed anytime.
  • A direct link to the invoice will be generated so you can send the pre-filled PDF link to their WhatsApp instantly.
